## Project Overview

This project analyzes Facebook Ads campaign data from **Globalshala**, a global education organization. The objective is to examine the performance of various ad campaigns in terms of engagement, cost-effectiveness, audience demographics, and regional performance using Excel-based pivot tables and pivot charts.

---

## Objectives

- Identify **underperforming campaigns** that should be discontinued.
- Understand **audience performance** by segment (Students vs. Educators).
- Analyze engagement across **age groups** and **geographies**.
- Provide **data-driven recommendations** for optimizing marketing spend.

---

## Data Source

- Internal dataset from Globalshala's **Facebook Ads Campaigns**.
- Data includes metrics such as impressions, clicks, amount spent, CTR, CPC, audience segments, age groups, and geographic targeting.

---

##  Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data analysis using Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts.

---

## Data Processing

- Cleaned and structured raw Excel data for pivot table use.
- Derived **CPC** using the formula:  
  `CPC = Amount Spent / Clicks`
- Segmented data by:
  - **Campaign**
  - **Audience**
  - **Age Group**
  - **Geography**
- Built pivot tables and charts to analyze each perspective independently.

##  Data Analysis & Visualization

### ✅ Campaign Performance
- Identified high and low-performing campaigns based on CTR and CPC.
- Campaigns like **Campaign 8 and 9** showed excellent CTR with low CPC.
- Campaigns like **Campaign 3 and 10** were cost-inefficient.

### ✅ Audience Performance
- **Students**: CTR = 5.10%, CPC = $0.90
- **Educators and Principals**: CTR = 2.73%, CPC = $1.92
- Students generated more clicks at a lower cost — clearly more efficient.

### ✅ Age Group Performance
- Highest engagement was observed in **13–17** and **18–24** age groups.
- Engagement significantly dropped after age 25.

### ✅ Geographic Performance
- Most cost-effective regions:
  - **India** ($0.42)
  - **Nigeria** ($0.73)
  - **Nepal** ($0.68)
- Least cost-effective:
  - **Canada** ($7.15)
  - **UK** ($7.08)
  - **USA** ($5.04)

---

## Insights & Recommendations

- **Discontinue or rework** Campaigns with high CPC and low CTR to reduce cost.
- **Focus on student audience**, which performs better in engagement and cost-efficiency.
- **Reallocate budget away from high-cost regions** like Canada and UK.
- **Target age groups 13–24**, where ads perform significantly better.
- **Increase ad spend in countries like India and Nigeria**, where clicks are cheaper and performance is strong.
